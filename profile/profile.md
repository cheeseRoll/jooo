## Per-job outputs the scorer must produce

- `fit_score` (0–100), `band`
- `geo_tag`: `BLR` | `ABROAD+VISA` | `ABROAD?VISA`
- `stage`: funding stage + last raise if known (web-check unknown companies)
- `why` — 2 lines max, specific, no fluff
- `resume_tips` — wording/emphasis tweaks for THIS job (e.g. "lead with the TWC model,
  rename 'Corporate performance analysis' bullet to mirror their 'business performance
  reporting' phrasing"). **Never fabricate experience.**
- `red_flags` — empty if none

## Cover letter voice (used post-Applied)

First person, confident but not salesy, concrete numbers from his experience, one line on
why THIS company (from real research), one line on the TWC model with the takeaway insight
(5-month store breakeven vs 45-month company cash-positive), close with a clear ask. Max
~180 words. No "I hope this email finds you well", no "esteemed organization".
